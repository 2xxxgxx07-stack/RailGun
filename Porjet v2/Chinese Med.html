Overview

HerbaMed is a clinic management system designed specifically for Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) practices. The project addresses the lack of digital tools tailored to TCM workflows, where standard clinic software fails to capture unique diagnostic modalities such as tongue analysis, pulse diagnosis, and herbal prescription management. Through user-centered design, HerbaMed aims to digitize the entire patient journey — from registration and consultation to prescription and inventory — within a unified, intuitive platform built for TCM practitioners.

UX Process

The design process began with research into how TCM practitioners manage patient records, diagnoses, and herbal prescriptions in their daily clinical workflow. Key pain points were identified around the complexity of documenting TCM-specific diagnoses and tracking multi-ingredient herbal formulas. These insights shaped the information architecture, user flows, and interface structure before being translated into wireframes and high-fidelity prototypes. Iterative testing ensured the solution aligned with real TCM clinical workflows.

Solution & Concept

HerbaMed brings all clinic operations into a single platform tailored for TCM. Practitioners can document tongue and pulse findings, apply pattern differentiation, and generate herbal prescriptions within a guided interface that mirrors the natural TCM consultation flow. Integrated inventory tracking ensures herb availability is always visible, reducing operational disruptions. The core concept is to make TCM-specific clinical documentation as simple and efficient as possible.

Design thinking

Empathize

Research focused on understanding how TCM practitioners conduct consultations, document diagnostic findings, and manage herbal prescriptions. Interviews revealed significant frustrations with generic clinic software that lacks fields for TCM-specific data. Practitioners often resorted to paper records or unstructured digital notes to capture tongue appearance, pulse quality, and pattern differentiation. User Personas were developed to represent TCM doctors and clinic administrators, capturing their goals, workflows, and primary pain points. Empathy Maps helped uncover the emotional and functional challenges practitioners face during consultations.

Define

From the research, three core problem areas were identified: the inability to document TCM-specific diagnostic data in existing systems, the complexity of managing multi-ingredient herbal prescriptions, and the challenge of tracking long-term treatment progress across multiple visits. Problem Statements were defined for each challenge, and Design Goals were established to guide the solution toward an interface that supports the natural TCM consultation flow. User Journey Maps captured the end-to-end patient experience from booking to prescription fulfillment.

Ideate

The ideation phase explored how to structure TCM-specific data within a clinic management system without overwhelming practitioners with complex forms. User Flows mapped the key interactions for TCM doctors and clinic staff, covering patient registration, consultation documentation, herbal prescription creation, and inventory management. A Sitemap organized the platform's features around the clinical workflow rather than administrative categories, ensuring that the most frequently used functions were easily accessible during consultations.

Prototype

Low-fidelity wireframes were first developed to validate the layout structure and navigation flow for key screens including the patient profile, TCM consultation form, herbal prescription builder, and inventory dashboard. High-fidelity designs then refined the visual language, incorporating a warm, earthy color palette to reflect the TCM identity while maintaining clinical professionalism. An interactive prototype was built to simulate the complete patient consultation workflow for user testing and stakeholder review.

Test

Usability testing was conducted with TCM practitioners to evaluate how well the interface supported real consultation workflows. Participants were asked to complete a full patient consultation scenario — from accessing the patient profile to generating an herbal prescription. Testing focused on identifying usability issues in the TCM diagnostic documentation flow and the prescription builder. Feedback was used to simplify complex form interactions and improve the clarity of TCM-specific terminology within the interface.

Testing Result & Feedback

  • Faster TCM Documentation — Practitioners completed consultation records significantly faster compared to their existing methods.
  • Reduced Prescription Errors — The guided herbal prescription builder reduced ingredient entry mistakes during testing.
  • Improved Clinical Flow — Users confirmed that the consultation interface matched their natural TCM diagnostic process.
  • Positive Inventory Visibility — Clinic staff appreciated having real-time herb stock levels visible during prescription creation.

Design System

Design System

The HerbaMed Design System uses a warm, earthy palette inspired by traditional herbal medicine to create a calm, trustworthy interface. It includes color tokens, typography, spacing, reusable components, and icon guidelines that maintain consistency across all clinic management workflows.

Mood and tone

HerbaMed's visual direction draws from the warmth and tradition of Chinese medicine, using earthy tones, natural textures, and clean layouts to balance the heritage of TCM with the clarity required in a clinical tool. The interface feels grounded, professional, and approachable — giving practitioners confidence while working through complex clinical documentation.
