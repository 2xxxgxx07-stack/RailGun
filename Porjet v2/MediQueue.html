MediQueue

MediQueue is a smart queue and scheduling platform connecting patients, doctors, and clinic staff to eliminate long hospital waiting times and streamline daily appointments. It brings transparency to the queue process and empowers both patients and staff with real-time information.

Overview

MediQueue is a queue management and appointment scheduling platform designed to reduce wait times and improve the overall patient experience at hospitals and clinics. The system connects patients, doctors, and administrative staff with real-time queue tracking, appointment booking, and automated notifications. The project focuses on eliminating manual queue processes, reducing patient anxiety during waiting, and providing operational analytics for clinic management.

UX Process

The design process started with research into how patients and clinic staff currently experience the queue and appointment system. Observations at hospital reception areas and interviews with patients, nurses, and administrative staff revealed pain points around uncertainty, manual coordination, and high workload. These insights were translated into user flows, information architecture, and interface concepts, with wireframes and prototypes developed and tested iteratively to ensure the solution served both patients and clinic operations.

Solution & Concept

MediQueue brings transparency and intelligence to the queue process. Patients can book appointments, track their queue position in real time, and receive proactive notifications about their turn — reducing unnecessary time spent waiting in the clinic. Clinic staff gain a centralized dashboard to monitor and manage queues across departments, track doctor availability, and handle walk-in patients alongside scheduled appointments. The concept centers on Transparent Waiting, Efficient Scheduling, and Streamlined Operations.

Design thinking

Empathize

Research explored the emotional and practical experience of patients waiting in hospital queues and the operational challenges faced by clinic administrative staff. Patients reported high anxiety from unpredictable wait times and a lack of information about their position in the queue. Staff described the difficulty of managing walk-in patients, appointment no-shows, and queue disruptions manually across multiple channels. User Personas were developed for patients, clinic staff, and doctors, each with distinct goals and frustrations. Empathy Maps revealed the emotional states and unmet needs that shaped the design priorities for MediQueue.

Define

Key challenges were defined for each user group. Patients needed real-time visibility into their queue position and estimated wait times. Staff needed tools to manage mixed appointment and walk-in queues without manual tracking across disconnected systems. Doctors needed to stay informed about their queue without disrupting their consultation flow. Problem Statements and Design Goals were established to guide the solution toward reducing wait time uncertainty, improving operational efficiency, and enabling proactive communication between patients and clinic staff. Journey Maps captured the full patient experience from booking to consultation.

Ideate

Ideation focused on how to make queue information immediately available to all stakeholders without requiring complex interactions. User Flows mapped the appointment booking journey for patients, the queue management workflow for staff, and the doctor's consultation readiness flow. A Sitemap organized the platform into a patient-facing mobile experience and a staff-facing web dashboard, ensuring that features were logically grouped by role and task rather than by system function. Key interactions such as queue notifications, appointment rescheduling, and doctor availability updates were designed to minimize manual intervention.

Prototype

Low-fidelity wireframes validated the layout and flow for the patient-facing queue tracker, appointment booking screens, and the staff queue management dashboard. High-fidelity designs introduced the visual design system, real-time status indicators, notification patterns, and queue number displays. An interactive prototype connected the patient booking flow, queue tracking view, and staff management dashboard to simulate a complete MediQueue experience for usability testing.

Test

Usability testing was conducted with patients and clinic staff using the prototype. Patient testing focused on the appointment booking flow, queue tracking visibility, and notification clarity. Staff testing evaluated how quickly team members could manage a combined walk-in and appointment queue. Feedback from both groups informed refinements to the notification timing, queue status display, and the interface for handling queue disruptions or doctor delays.

Testing Result & Feedback

  • Reduced Patient Anxiety — Real-time queue visibility significantly reduced patient uncertainty about waiting times.
  • Improved Staff Efficiency — Staff were able to manage queues faster using the centralized dashboard compared to manual methods.
  • Clear Queue Status — Patients and staff confirmed that queue position and estimated wait times were easy to understand.
  • Effective Notifications — Proactive alerts helped patients return to the clinic at the right time, reducing no-shows.

Design System

Design System

The MediQueue Design System supports both a patient-facing mobile interface and a staff-facing web dashboard. It includes color tokens, typography, spacing, queue status components, notification patterns, and interactive elements designed to communicate real-time information clearly across both platforms.

Mood and tone

MediQueue's visual direction is calm, clear, and reassuring. Since the platform is used by patients experiencing the stress of waiting, the design uses a clean layout, clear status indicators, and approachable visual language to reduce anxiety and build confidence in the process. The staff interface uses a more functional, data-dense layout optimized for operational clarity and speed.
